# :page_facing_up: Contributor Code of Conduct.

## Our Pledge
We as contributors and maintainers of Dynaserve CLI pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.  

We commit to acting professionally, respectfully, and inclusively at all times.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior:  
- Using welcoming and inclusive language  
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints  
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully  
- Focusing on what’s best for the project  
- Showing empathy and courtesy to other community members  

Unacceptable behavior includes:  
- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language  
- Personal attacks, threats, or insults  
- Trolling or disruptive behavior  
- Publishing private information without consent  

## Reporting
If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainers at **security@dynaserve.com**. Reports will be handled confidentially and investigated promptly.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when representing Dynaserve CLI.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban participants, as necessary to enforce this Code of Conduct.
